[h1]Apocalipse Music Pack - Build 42[/h1]

Music-radio foundation for ApocalipseBRRadio. Adds a station at 94.2 FM, separate song registrations, compact radio commands, timed local lyrics, and between-song radio talk.

Requires apocalipsebrradio. This template contains no music files; add songs using tools/import-song.mjs before distributing a music catalog.
